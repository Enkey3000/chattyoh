# Product Vision

**Project:** ChattyOh Inc · **Version:** 0.1 (draft) · **Owner:** Product Owner · **Status:** Draft
**Upstream:** `problem-statement.md` · **Feeds:** `01-prd.md`, `roadmap.md`

## 1. Vision

**One agent-assisted workspace that takes an idea from conversation to a deployed, documented and tested product, with the human always in control.**

## 2. Mission

Give every builder a single, trustworthy place to chat, plan, design, document, build, test, review, release and automate, where AI works alongside people, is clearly labeled, and never acts beyond what people approve.

## 3. Positioning statement

For solo makers and small teams who turn ideas into products, ChattyOh is an agent-assisted project workspace that keeps one shared context from first conversation to release. Unlike general chat assistants or separate builders and trackers, ChattyOh puts approvals, limits, audit and honest labeling into the workflow itself.

## 4. Product principles

| # | Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Human in control** | Sensitive actions need visible approval; limits are enforced; everything is audited (PRD-G-02) |
| 2 | **Honest by default** | Simulated or preview features are labeled; no fake results; claims match reality (PRD-G-03) |
| 3 | **One context** | Chat, specs, tasks, agents and releases share the same project |
| 4 | **Safe with your data** | No silent loss; secrets never exposed; tenant isolation |
| 5 | **Accessible to everyone** | WCAG 2.2 AA; keyboard and screen-reader friendly |
| 6 | **Same product everywhere** | One codebase for web, desktop and mobile (PRD-G-04) |
| 7 | **Start small, grow safely** | Walking skeleton first; each slice shippable |

## 5. Value by persona

| Persona | Value |
|---|---|
| Maker | From idea to first project in minutes, with AI help that is easy to review |
| Team lead | Shared plan, roles and gates that actually stop bad releases |
| Admin / security reviewer | Clear audit trail and approvals for AI and data actions |
| Agency lead | Repeatable process and consistent quality across clients |

## 6. What ChattyOh is and is not

| It is | It is not (for MVP) |
|---|---|
| A workspace for planning, writing, tracking and controlled automation | A hosting platform or app marketplace |
| A place where AI drafts and humans approve | An autonomous system that acts without approval |
| One product on web, desktop and mobile | A set of separate apps |
| Honest about what is real and what is preview | A demo that implies features it does not have |

## 7. Horizons

| Horizon | Theme | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| H1 — MVP (weeks 0–16) | Trustworthy core | Accounts, projects, chat, documents, agents with approvals, audit, safe data, accessible UI |
| H2 — Growth (next 6–12 months) | Real integrations | Source control, real test execution, deployment targets, SSO, billing, richer collaboration |
| H3 — Platform (12+ months) | Ecosystem | Agent marketplace, templates, partner integrations, native mobile UI, enterprise controls |

## 8. North-star and guardrail metrics (proposed)

North star: **weekly active workspaces that complete at least one approved deliverable** (document approved or agent run reviewed). Guardrails: activation within 10 minutes (PRD-M-01), agent success within limits (PRD-M-04), zero open Critical/High integrity or accessibility defects at release (PRD-M-06).

## 9. Brand and experience direction

Calm, confident and clear. Visual identity follows the logo palette (deep navy, royal blue, cyan, steel gray) with light and dark themes. Plain language; every automated or simulated element is visibly marked.

## 10. Risks to the vision

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Scope grows into "everything tool" | MVP scope filter; non-goals enforced |
| AI cost or quality undermines trust | Quotas, labeling, human review |
| Marketing outruns product | Claims review gate before release (REL-11) |
| Competing products copy features | Differentiate on trust: approvals, audit, honesty |
